Data quality & interpretation

— filtered records have conflicting source status fields. Details show both values. These counts are not a measure of planning performance.

Counts use the application’s recorded municipality, not a spatial join. Municipal boundaries are simplified for display. Islands and separate jurisdiction boundaries must not be interpreted as utility coverage.

Current public evidence cannot establish site-level electricity, water or wastewater capacity. No readiness score or infrastructure dependency is inferred.
