How this was measured

  1. Select source records labelled Vaughan.
  2. Use their full original geometry.
  3. Project into NAD83 / UTM zone 17N (metres).
  4. Measure minimum distance to five published construction-line features intersecting Vaughan.
  5. Assign each record once to a fixed 1 km grid using an interior point.

250 m, 500 m and 1,000 m are analyst-selected screening distances, not utility standards. Crossing a line does not prove connection.
